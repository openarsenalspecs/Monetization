# InceptID

**Know the Source. Own the Story.**

InceptID is an open-source AI provenance and authenticity framework designed to trace images, videos, audio, and text back to their original source across platforms. It reconstructs content lineage, detects manipulation, and preserves digital authorship in an age of AI-generated and rapidly reposted media.

---

## Overview

InceptID builds a universal provenance layer for the internet. It tracks how content is created, modified, and shared across platforms using AI-driven fingerprinting, steganographic embedding, and cross-platform intelligence.

The system is modular, extensible, and designed for federated deployment under an AGPL-3.0+ license to ensure openness and prevent closed-source capture.
