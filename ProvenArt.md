# ProvenArt

**Honoring human originality, one piece at a time.**

---

## Overview

ProvenArt is an open-source platform designed to empower human creators by verifying, protecting, and monetizing original works across the internet. It supports images, video, audio, and artwork while enabling creators to set their own pricing and licensing terms.

The system is designed so that any platform—social media, personal websites, or hosting services—can act as a **distribution layer for human-created content**, while ProvenArt manages ownership, attribution, licensing, and monetization.

AI-generated content is not treated as independent authorship. If AI tools are used, the licensing and ownership are tied to the **human prompt author**, and must be packaged as part of the creation record.

---

## Core Vision

- Enable universal distribution of human creativity
- Remove dependency on centralized storefronts
- Allow creators to retain ownership across all platforms
- Ensure fair compensation through transparent licensing
- Build a system where content carries its rights wherever it goes

## Full Feature List

### 1. Creator Identity & Profiles
- Creator accounts with verification for human authorship
- Portfolio pages showcasing all works
- Revenue tracking dashboard (earnings, downloads, views)
- Custom branding for creator profiles (logos, themes, layouts)
- Multi-language support
- Creator mentorship system for onboarding new artists

---

### 2. Content Upload & Management
- Supports images, video, audio, text, and mixed media
- Metadata fields: title, description, tags, medium, category
- Ownership and licensing assignment at upload time
- Batch uploads for collections or portfolios
- Version control for updating existing works
- Optional watermarking (visible or invisible)
- File hashing for integrity and authenticity tracking
- Creator destination links embedded per content item

---

### 3. Creator Destination Links
- Add external links to each profile or individual work
- Supports websites, social media, portfolios, and storefronts
- Click tracking and analytics for outbound traffic
- Optional display rules (public, post-purchase, or restricted visibility)
- Helps creators maintain independent presence beyond ProvenArt

---

### 4. Licensing & Rights Management
Creators can choose flexible licensing models:

- Attribution-based sharing license
- MIT-style reuse license with attribution
- Commercial usage license with automated royalties
- Limited edition / scarcity-based licensing
- Subscription-based access models

AI-assisted content:
- Ownership assigned to prompt author
- Licensing is bundled with creation metadata
- Revenue flows directly to prompt creator

All licensing is enforced through platform-level metadata and payment rules.
