# AnchorID

**Identity-powered sponsorships.**

AnchorID is a privacy-preserving creator monetization and sponsorship network where users earn through tips and product-based sponsorship identity layers. Instead of traditional ads, creators intentionally select products to align with their identity, and brands pay for verified exposure through impressions, engagement, and campaign performance.

---

## Core Concept

AnchorID transforms the creator economy into an identity-driven sponsorship system:

- Creators choose products they want to represent
- Brands fund campaigns tied to those products
- Revenue is generated through verified impressions and engagement
- Tips remain separate as voluntary appreciation
- All financial reporting is encrypted and user-controlled

---

## Key Features

### 👤 Identity & KYC System
- Real identity verification via trusted KYC providers
- Liveness checks and government ID validation
- Annual re-verification (liveliness checks)
- Compliance-ready onboarding (AML-aware structure)
- Role-based accounts (creator, advertiser, hybrid)

---

### 🧃 Product Sponsorship Identity Layer
- Users select products to sponsor on their profile
- Example: Kleenex tissues, Dove skincare, Apple accessories
- Sponsored product becomes part of creator identity page
- No traditional ad slots—only intentional product alignment
- Tiered sponsorship identity support:
  - Primary sponsor (core identity)
  - Secondary sponsor (support layer)
  - Rotational sponsor (time-based campaigns)

---

### 📢 Brand Campaign Builder
- Self-serve advertiser dashboard
- Create product sponsorship campaigns
- Upload creative assets (images, messaging, branding)
- Set budgets and payout models (CPM, CPC, flat sponsorship)
- Define targeting filters based on creator categories
- Real-time campaign performance tracking

---

### 💸 Monetization Engine
- Tips (voluntary support payments)
- Sponsorship revenue (product-based earnings)
- Impression-based payouts
- Engagement-weighted revenue distribution
- Platform fees on tips and sponsorship transactions

---

### 📊 Ledger & Financial Tracking System
- Immutable event-based ledger
- Tracks:
  - tips
  - impressions
  - sponsorship earnings
  - marketplace transactions
- Clear separation between income types
- Audit-ready financial structure

---

### 🧠 AI Optimization Engine
- AI-driven sponsorship matching
- Revenue prediction per product and creator
- Automated campaign optimization suggestions
- Dynamic pricing insights for sponsorship placements
- Creator growth and monetization forecasting

---

### 🛡️ Fraud + Integrity Engine
- Bot detection and invalid traffic filtering
- Device fingerprint clustering
- Geo-velocity anomaly detection
- Earnings manipulation prevention
- Risk scoring per user and campaign
- Fraud-resistant payout validation system

---

### 🔁 Secondary Sponsorship Marketplace
- Trade sponsorship slots between users and brands
- Auction-based or direct transfer model
- Dynamic pricing discovery for sponsorship placements
- Creates liquidity in creator sponsorship identity slots
- Platform fee applied to marketplace transactions

---

### 🧾 Tax & Reporting System (Encrypted)
- Monthly earnings reports (tips vs sponsorship income separated)
- Tax-ready summaries for creators
- All reports encrypted client-side (E2EE)
- User-controlled decryption of financial data
- Exportable audit and reporting formats

---

### 🔐 Privacy & Security Model (E2EE)
- End-to-end encrypted financial reports
- Client-side encryption for sensitive user data
- Server only processes non-sensitive metadata
- Wallet and identity data protected via encryption layer
- Privacy-first architecture for financial sovereignty

---

## System Architecture Overview

AnchorID consists of:

- Identity & KYC layer
- Sponsorship identity system
- Campaign marketplace engine
- Ledger & financial system
- Fraud & integrity engine
- AI optimization layer
- Secondary sponsorship marketplace
- Encrypted reporting system
