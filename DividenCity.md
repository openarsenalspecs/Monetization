## Overview

DividenCity is an open-source platform for implementing transparent, resource-backed public dividend programs at the municipal, county, state, tribal, territorial, and federal levels. The platform enables governments to track revenue generated from publicly owned assets, automatically calculate citizen dividends, and distribute payments to verified residents.

DividenCity is based on the principle that when public assets generate private profits, a portion of that value should be returned to the people through an auditable, transparent dividend system. The platform supports recurring dividend programs funded by natural resource royalties, commercial use of public property, concession agreements, infrastructure leases, licensing revenue, government budget surpluses, and other approved public revenue sources.

Built with a modular architecture, DividenCity allows each jurisdiction to define its own policies while remaining compatible with larger state or federal implementations.
