## Overview

YieldGrid is an open-source participation economy framework that enables organizations to build transparent, equitable, and sustainable profit-sharing systems based on verified participation rather than popularity or arbitrary rankings.

Built around the principle of **Equal Pay for Equal Work**, YieldGrid provides an AI-powered recommendation engine that analyzes an organization's products, services, workflows, revenue streams, and participation models to design fair compensation frameworks.

Rather than being limited to social media, YieldGrid is designed as a universal participation infrastructure that can be deployed across virtually any industry.
