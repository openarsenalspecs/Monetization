# Quantara

### Creator First Advertising.

Quantara is an open-source AGPL 3.0+ advertising marketplace that connects businesses and creators through transparent, permission-based partnerships. Businesses create campaigns with clear terms, and creators choose exactly which brands, products, and categories they want to promote across websites, blogs, newsletters, and social platforms.

Quantara replaces forced ad networks with a creator-controlled ecosystem built on trust, fairness, and measurable performance.
